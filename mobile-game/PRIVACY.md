# Privacy Policy – Star Dodge

Star Dodge does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. The game works offline, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your best score is saved only on your own device (local storage) and is removed when you uninstall the app.

The game makes no network requests and does not request internet access or sensitive Android permissions.
