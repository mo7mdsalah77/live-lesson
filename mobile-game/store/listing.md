**Full description (max 4000):**
Star Dodge is a fast, simple arcade game you can play with one thumb.

Drag to steer your ship, grab the golden stars and dodge the red blocks falling from above. Every star is a point. The longer you survive, the faster it gets – how high can you score?

FEATURES
• One-thumb controls – just drag
• Gets faster and harder as you play
• Saves your best score
• Punchy sound effects and vibration (sound can be switched off in-game)
• Works fully offline
• No ads, no purchases, no tracking

Quick to learn, hard to put down. Download Star Dodge and chase your best score!
