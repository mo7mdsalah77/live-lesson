# Privacy Policy – Who is the Imposter? / مين برا السالفة

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It works fully offline, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Player names you type, and your settings (language, timer, game type), are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app makes no network requests and does not request internet access or any sensitive Android permission.
