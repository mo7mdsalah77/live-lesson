# يلا يا أسطى / Yalla Osta

An original, easy and funny driving game: you are a microbus driver on a busy ring road. Drag to steer, pick up waiting riders for fare, dodge cars and tuk-tuks, hit the horn (📯) to make slow cars move over, and watch out for speed bumps. Three hearts, day-to-night cycle, Egyptian-flavoured speech bubbles and signs. Arabic (default) and English.

Controls: drag anywhere / arrow keys to steer, horn button or Space to honk, Esc to pause.
Run: open `www/index.html`. Tests: `node --test tests/logic.test.cjs`; headless playthrough: `NODE_PATH=../node_modules node tests/e2e.cjs`.
Android: same Capacitor packaging as `../mafia-game` (`webDir: www`).
