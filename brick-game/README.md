# طوب ومسطرة / Bricks & Ruler

An original take on the classic brick-breaker: slide a ruler under the ball and smash the bricks. Where the ball hits the ruler sets its angle. Multi-hit and unbreakable bricks, 8 level patterns that loop with tougher bricks, and falling gifts: longer ruler, multi-ball, slow ball, extra life. Three lives. Arabic (default) and English.

Controls: drag or move the mouse to slide the ruler, tap / Space to launch, Esc to pause.
Run: open `www/index.html`. Tests: `node --test tests/logic.test.cjs`; bot playthrough: `NODE_PATH=../node_modules node tests/e2e.cjs`.
Android: same Capacitor packaging as `../mafia-game` (`webDir: www`).
